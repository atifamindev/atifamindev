### 👨‍💻 About Me

- 📱 **Senior Mobile Developer** focused on Android, iOS, Flutter, and production mobile systems.
- 💳 Experienced with **SoftPOS payments, refunds, voids, reports, secure sessions, PIN/biometric login, and SDK integrations**.
- 🧠 Built identity and AI-assisted flows including **OCR, NFC chip reading, face matching, and liveness checks**.
- 🧩 I care about **clean architecture, maintainable code, performance, and predictable app behavior**.
- 🚀 Worked with **Kotlin, Swift, Flutter, Jetpack Compose, UIKit, ARKit, RealityKit, Agora RTC, Firebase, NestJS, PostgreSQL, and Prisma**.
- 🔍 I enjoy **debugging hard issues, improving app flows, and turning business requirements into reliable products**.
- 💡 My focus is shipping mobile products that are polished, secure, scalable, and ready for real users.
